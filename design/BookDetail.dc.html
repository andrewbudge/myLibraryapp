About this book

Trauma is a fact of life. Veterans and their families deal with the painful aftermath of combat; one in five Americans has been molested; one in four grew up with alcoholics. Dr. Bessel van der Kolk, one of the world's foremost experts on trauma, has spent over three decades working with survivors — exploring how trauma reshapes both body and brain, and offering new paths to recovery.
